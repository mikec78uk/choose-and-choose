CHOOSE & CHOOSE

Claiming the offer on the product page

One "Unlock your gift" box sits in the buy box on every qualifying product page. What's inside it depends on how much the customer has to choose. In every scenario, products go straight into the basket, the slots show what's already there, and gifts can be chosen at any time.

  1. 1

    One qualifying product, a few gifts

    Buy 2 of this product, choose 1 of 4 gifts. The gifts are shown right in the box with a "Choose" button.

    DesktopMobile

  2. 2

    Several qualifying products, one gift

    Buy any 2 of 6 products, get one gift. The box lists the products; the gift is added automatically. Starts with one item already in the basket.

    DesktopMobile

  3. 3

    Several qualifying products, several gifts

    Buy any 2 of 10 products, choose up to 2 of 10 gifts. The box shows progress and a summary; the choosing happens in a drawer. Starts with one item already in the basket.

    DesktopMobile

All names, prices, images and dates are placeholders.
